# search-v1 licence

The questions in `val.jsonl` and `test.jsonl` and the documents under `extra/` are synthetic.
The extra documents are generated by `synth/vault_synth` (sets `search-x1`, seed 11, and
`search-x2`, seed 12); none belongs to a real person, account or organisation. Identity pages
are marked SPECIMEN, Aadhaar-style numbers fail the Verhoeff checksum, GSTINs fail the GSTIN
checksum, any other 12-digit identifier fails Verhoeff and any 13 to 19 digit identifier fails
Luhn, and card numbers are the payment networks' published test numbers. PANs, passports, voter
cards, licences and certificates are real-shaped but follow a pattern only this project uses (a
leading ZZ); nobody reserves that pattern, so a real number could look the same by chance.

The questions also refer to the eval-v1 documents in `../eval-v1`, which carry their own
licence file.

Released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The same fonts and the same Kannada limit as eval-v1 apply (see `../eval-v1/LICENSE.md`).

## What is committed

Only the clean variant of the `val` and `test` splits of each extra set, with its truth files,
and the two question files. The `train` split is not committed; regenerate everything with
`make search-set`.
